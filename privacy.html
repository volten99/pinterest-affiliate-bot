Privacy Policy

Pinterest Affiliate Bot ("the App") is a personal tool for creating and scheduling Pinterest pins linking to affiliate products.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect personal data from end users. It stores only: Pinterest access tokens and board IDs you provide, and the affiliate product data you configure.

How Data Is Used

Access tokens are used solely to authenticate with the Pinterest API to create pins. Product data is used to generate pin images and descriptions. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Third-Party Services

The App interacts with the Pinterest API and with affiliate partner websites (via links in pins). Their privacy policies apply to interactions with those services.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on the user's own device. No remote database or cloud storage is used by the App.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the app owner.
